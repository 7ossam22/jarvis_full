# Daily Roasting Schedule

Roasting runs Tuesday through Saturday, 6:00am to 1:00pm, on the 12kg Probat drum.
Mornings start with the lightest roast profile (washed Ethiopia) while the drum is
still coming up to temperature stability, then step up through medium roasts, and
finish the day on the darker espresso blends once the machine has fully soaked heat.

See [[Equipment Maintenance Log]] for pre-shift checks — a cold drum or dirty
thermocouple throws off the first two batches every time. Batch sizes are capped
at 10kg green to leave room for even bean rotation. Each batch gets logged against
the [[Quality Control Checklist]] before it's approved for packaging.

Sunday and Monday are reserved for [[Equipment Maintenance Log]] deep cleans and
green bean receiving from [[Ethiopia Yirgacheffe Supplier]] and
[[Colombia Huila Supplier]] shipments.
