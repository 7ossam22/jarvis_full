# Warehouse Layout

Green bean storage sits on the north wall, organized by supplier and harvest date —
[[Ethiopia Yirgacheffe Supplier]] lots on the top shelves since they move fastest,
[[Colombia Huila Supplier]] lots below. FIFO is enforced with painter's tape date
labels on every bag.

Roasted, unpackaged coffee never sits more than 48 hours before bagging — degassing
happens in sealed valve bags per the [[Quality Control Checklist]] procedure.
Packaging materials from the [[Packaging Supplier Contract]] are stored on the south
wall next to the labeling station. Finished goods for the [[Farmers Market Strategy]]
pop-up crates live by the loading door for easy Saturday morning pickup.
