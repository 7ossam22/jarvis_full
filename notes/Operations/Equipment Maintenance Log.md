# Equipment Maintenance Log

The Probat drum roaster gets a full teardown clean every Monday: chaff collector,
cooling tray mesh, and thermocouple calibration against a reference probe. Espresso
machines on the tasting bar are backflushed daily and descaled monthly.

Grinder burrs (used for cupping and the tasting bar, referenced in
[[Quality Control Checklist]]) are swapped every 400kg throughput. Log every service
here with date, technician, and parts used so [[Cash Flow Forecast]] can plan for
replacement burrs and heating elements before they fail mid-batch.

Any unplanned downtime gets flagged to the team in the [[Weekly Team Meeting Notes]].
