# Quality Control Checklist

Every roast batch is cupped within 24 hours using the SCA cupping protocol: fragrance,
aroma, flavor, aftertaste, acidity, body, balance, uniformity, clean cup, sweetness.
Anything scoring under 82 gets pulled and reviewed against the roast log from the
[[Daily Roasting Schedule]].

Packaging checks: seal integrity, one-way valve function, correct label for the
[[Seasonal Blend - Autumn Harvest]] or [[Single Origin Lineup]] SKU, and roast date
stamp. Random weight checks on 1 in 20 bags to catch scale drift.

Any batch that fails cupping twice in a row triggers a call to the supplier —
cross-reference [[Supplier Vetting Process]] before re-ordering that lot.
