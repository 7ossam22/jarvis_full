# Q3 Financial Summary

Revenue for Q3 came in at $184,000, up 12% over Q2, driven mostly by wholesale
accounts and the [[Farmers Market Strategy]] pop-up circuit. Cost of goods sold
held steady at 38% thanks to locked-in pricing from [[Ethiopia Yirgacheffe Supplier]]
and [[Colombia Huila Supplier]].

Net margin landed at 14%, slightly above the 11% target set at the start of the year.
See [[Pricing Strategy]] for the reasoning behind the fall price increase, and
[[Cash Flow Forecast]] for how this quarter changes the runway. Payroll (see
[[Staff Roles and Responsibilities]]) remains the largest fixed cost at 31% of revenue.
