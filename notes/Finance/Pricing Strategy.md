# Pricing Strategy

Retail bags are priced at $19 for 12oz, positioned just above the regional average
to reflect the direct-trade story on [[Ethiopia Yirgacheffe Supplier]] and
[[Colombia Huila Supplier]] lots. Wholesale pricing is tiered by volume, reviewed
quarterly alongside the [[Q3 Financial Summary]].

A 6% price increase went into effect this fall to offset rising green coffee costs
and the new [[Packaging Supplier Contract]] rates. The [[Loyalty Program Design]]
exists partly to soften that increase for repeat customers by rewarding volume
instead of just cutting price.
