# Vendor Payment Terms

Green coffee suppliers are paid net-30 against bill of lading, standard for both
[[Ethiopia Yirgacheffe Supplier]] and [[Colombia Huila Supplier]] contracts. The
[[Packaging Supplier Contract]] runs net-45 with a 2% early-payment discount we
almost always take.

Late payments have never happened, but the [[Cash Flow Forecast]] keeps a 30-day
buffer specifically to protect these relationships — supplier trust is worth more
than the float. Review terms annually alongside the [[Supplier Vetting Process]].
