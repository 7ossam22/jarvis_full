# Cash Flow Forecast

Twelve-month rolling forecast shows healthy runway through next summer, assuming
the growth trend from the [[Q3 Financial Summary]] holds. Biggest seasonal dip is
January-February after holiday wholesale orders taper off, offset by planning
inventory buys from [[Vendor Payment Terms]] schedules around that dip.

Capital reserve covers three months of [[Staff Roles and Responsibilities]] payroll
plus a buffer for [[Equipment Maintenance Log]] replacement parts. The
[[Pricing Strategy]] increase this fall was modeled directly into this forecast.
