# Cold Brew Recipe

1:8 coffee-to-water ratio, coarse grind, steeped 16 hours at room temperature using
a batch of the [[Seasonal Blend - Autumn Harvest]] or whatever [[Single Origin
Lineup]] coffee is currently in stock. Filtered through a paper cone before
kegging.

Served on tap at the tasting bar and in bottles at [[Farmers Market Strategy]]
stalls during warmer months. Batch size is tied to weekend sales forecasts logged
in the [[Weekly Team Meeting Notes]]. Kegs are cleaned per the same schedule as
espresso equipment in [[Equipment Maintenance Log]].
