# Seasonal Blend - Autumn Harvest

A honey-process [[Colombia Huila Supplier]] lot blended with a small percentage of
natural-process Brazil for body, roasted medium per the
[[Daily Roasting Schedule]]. Notes of caramel, baked apple, and toasted pecan.

Released each September and featured across the [[Instagram Content Calendar]] and
[[Farmers Market Strategy]] stalls. [[Loyalty Program Design]] members get early
access a week before general release. Cupping scores are tracked in the
[[Quality Control Checklist]] log like every other batch.
