# Brand Voice Guidelines

Nova Roasters speaks like a knowledgeable friend, not a lecture. Favor plain
language over tasting-note jargon on anything customer-facing — save "notes of
stone fruit and bergamot" for the [[Single Origin Lineup]] bag copy, not social
captions.

Every post in the [[Instagram Content Calendar]] should answer "why should someone
who's never heard of us care?" Consistency matters more than cleverness. When in
doubt about a supplier story, check facts against [[Ethiopia Yirgacheffe Supplier]]
or [[Colombia Huila Supplier]] notes before publishing.
