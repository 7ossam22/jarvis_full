# Instagram Content Calendar

Three posts a week: Monday roast-day behind-the-scenes (tie into
[[Daily Roasting Schedule]]), Wednesday origin story spotlighting
[[Ethiopia Yirgacheffe Supplier]] or [[Colombia Huila Supplier]], and Friday
product feature for the [[Seasonal Blend - Autumn Harvest]] or
[[Single Origin Lineup]].

Tone follows the [[Brand Voice Guidelines]] — warm, specific, never salesy. Stories
highlight [[Farmers Market Strategy]] pop-up locations each Saturday morning, and
customer photos get reposted to build the [[Loyalty Program Design]] community feel.
