# Farmers Market Strategy

We run three Saturday markets a month, rotating between the downtown, riverside,
and college-town locations. Each stall pours drip and cold brew samples featuring
the current [[Seasonal Blend - Autumn Harvest]] and sells retail bags at the same
price as [[Pricing Strategy]] retail.

Setup uses the crate system from [[Warehouse Layout]] for fast Saturday loading.
Market sales feed directly into [[Q3 Financial Summary]] as a growing revenue line,
and market regulars are the first people invited into the [[Loyalty Program Design]]
pilot.
