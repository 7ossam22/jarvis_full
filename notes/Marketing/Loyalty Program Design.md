# Loyalty Program Design

Punch-card style: buy 10 bags, get the 11th free, tracked digitally through the POS
so it works both in the tasting room and at [[Farmers Market Strategy]] stalls.
Designed as a response to the fall [[Pricing Strategy]] increase — reward loyalty
with value instead of discounting the whole line.

Members get early access to [[Seasonal Blend - Autumn Harvest]] releases and a
birthday-month free drink. Program performance is reviewed alongside the
[[Instagram Content Calendar]] since most sign-ups currently come from Instagram
story links.
