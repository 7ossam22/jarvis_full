# Training - Espresso Machine

Certification has three stages: dial-in (grind, dose, shot time), milk texturing,
and latte art basics. Trainees practice on the tasting bar machine during slow
mid-morning hours, logged as part of the [[Onboarding Checklist]].

Espresso used for training pulls from the current [[Single Origin Lineup]] rotation
so staff learn to taste the difference a roast profile makes — ties directly back
to the [[Quality Control Checklist]] cupping vocabulary. Certified staff get listed
in [[Staff Roles and Responsibilities]] as tasting-bar qualified.
