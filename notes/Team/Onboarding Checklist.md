# Onboarding Checklist

New hires shadow a full shift before touching the espresso machine solo — pair
with a senior barista per [[Staff Roles and Responsibilities]]. Day one covers
[[Training - Espresso Machine]] fundamentals, food safety certification, and a
walkthrough of the [[Warehouse Layout]].

By week two, new team members should be able to explain the difference between
the [[Seasonal Blend - Autumn Harvest]] and the [[Single Origin Lineup]] to a
customer unprompted. Checklist completion is tracked and reviewed in the first
[[Weekly Team Meeting Notes]] after their start date.
