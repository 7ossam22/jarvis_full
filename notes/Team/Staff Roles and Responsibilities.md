# Staff Roles and Responsibilities

Roasting is a dedicated role — currently one head roaster following the
[[Daily Roasting Schedule]] and signing off on [[Quality Control Checklist]]
results. Baristas rotate across the tasting bar and market pop-ups per the
[[Farmers Market Strategy]] schedule.

Admin covers [[Vendor Payment Terms]], scheduling, and social media planning
against the [[Instagram Content Calendar]]. All roles get cross-trained on
[[Training - Espresso Machine]] basics so the tasting bar never goes understaffed
during a call-out.
