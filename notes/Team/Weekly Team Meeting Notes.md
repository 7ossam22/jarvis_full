# Weekly Team Meeting Notes

Standing Monday 9am meeting. Recurring agenda: roast schedule changes from
[[Daily Roasting Schedule]], any [[Equipment Maintenance Log]] downtime from the
prior week, upcoming [[Farmers Market Strategy]] staffing, and a read of recent
[[Customer Feedback - September]] themes.

New hires get introduced here per the [[Onboarding Checklist]], and anyone
finishing [[Training - Espresso Machine]] certification gets called out. Action
items are assigned by name and reviewed at the top of the following week's meeting.
