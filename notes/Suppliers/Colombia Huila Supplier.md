# Colombia Huila Supplier

A family-run farm cooperative in Huila supplying washed and honey-process lots.
Honey lots are the backbone of the [[Seasonal Blend - Autumn Harvest]] for their
caramel sweetness; washed lots go into the [[Single Origin Lineup]].

Same [[Vendor Payment Terms]] as our other green coffee vendors, net-30 against
bill of lading. Passed [[Supplier Vetting Process]] review last spring with no
issues flagged. Shipments land quarterly and get logged into the
[[Warehouse Layout]] south racks on arrival.
