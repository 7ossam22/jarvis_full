the Gedeo zone, in operation with us for four seasons now. Lots are washed process,
floral and citrus-forward, and typically roasted light per the
[[Daily Roasting Schedule]] to preserve that character.

Payment follows standard [[Vendor Payment Terms]]. This origin anchors the
