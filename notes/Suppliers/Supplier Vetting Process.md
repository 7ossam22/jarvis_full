# Supplier Vetting Process

New green coffee suppliers go through a three-step vet: sample cupping against the
[[Quality Control Checklist]] rubric, a traceability check back to the farm or
cooperative, and a reference call with another roaster who buys from them.

Only after passing all three does a supplier move to [[Vendor Payment Terms]]
negotiation. Both [[Ethiopia Yirgacheffe Supplier]] and [[Colombia Huila Supplier]]
went through this process and are re-vetted annually to make sure quality and
practices haven't slipped.
