# Packaging Supplier Contract

Compostable one-way-valve bags sourced from a regional packaging vendor, contract
renewed annually. Rates increased 4% this cycle, factored into the fall
[[Pricing Strategy]] review and the [[Cash Flow Forecast]].

Payment terms are net-45 with a 2% early-pay discount, tracked in
[[Vendor Payment Terms]]. Minimum order quantities are sized to six months of
usage based on current [[Q3 Financial Summary]] volume, stored on the south wall
per [[Warehouse Layout]].
